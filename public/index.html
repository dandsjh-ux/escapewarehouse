Warehouse

Sneak through the dark warehouse, collect the words you need to build your sentence, then run for the exit before the seeker tags you.

or join a friend's game

How to play
  • Hiders get a sentence. Find each word in the right room and press E to pick it up.
  • When your sentence is complete, the EXITS turn green. There's one on each floor – reach either to escape!
  • Press E next to a low crate or sandbags to climb over them.
  • The Seeker counts first, then hunts. Touch a hider to tag them. Watch out for the locator ping!
  • Hide behind crates (press C to crouch) or inside lockers.
  • 3+ players: tagged hiders go to Prison. A free friend can open the door to rescue them.
  • 2 players: a tag wins the round instantly, then you swap roles.
